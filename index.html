About Häyhä Floats

Häyhä Floats is a small, Utah-based rental company focused on Nordic-style sauna and lake experiences. We operate as a pickup-only service from our Provo location – you tow the boat, we provide the heat.

How It Works

1. Book Your Date

Send us your preferred date, lake, and group size. We’ll confirm availability and final pricing.

2. Pick Up in Provo

Arrive at our Provo office with a capable tow vehicle and hitch. We’ll walk you through safety, launch tips, and sauna operation.

3. Tow, Launch, Relax

You tow the boat to a legal Utah lake or reservoir, launch, enjoy your session, then return the boat and trailer to us.
