About Häyhä Floats

Häyhä Floats is a small, Utah-based rental company focused on Nordic-style sauna and lake experiences. Inspired by floating saunas in Sweden, we’re bringing that same slow-down, breathe-deep rhythm to Utah.

We operate from a Provo warehouse/office. You tow the boat to any legal Utah lake or reservoir, launch, enjoy your session, then return it to us.
