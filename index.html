Welcome to Häyhä Floats

Utah’s First Floating Sauna Experience.

Häyhä Floats is a Deer Creek-based sauna & lake company inspired by Scandinavian sauna culture. You and your crew meet us at Deer Creek, walk down to the dock, and step aboard a custom floating sauna barge.


Each session blends hot sauna rounds with cold immersion, music, and time to just be with your people — no distractions, just mountains, water, and steam.

Session details

What’s included

• 90-minute private booking at Deer Creek
• Pre-heated wood-fired sauna
• Cool-down deck + lake access for cold plunges (you choose if you’re getting in)
• Safety walkthrough and dockside support
• BYO towels, water, snacks, & playlist.

Heat, view, repeat.
