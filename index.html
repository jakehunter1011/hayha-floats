About Häyhä Floats

Häyhä Floats is a Utah-based floating sauna project inspired by Nordic sauna culture and long lake days in Sweden. We’re bringing that same calm, slow, intentional vibe to Deer Creek Reservoir.

Every session is guided meet-up style: you meet us at Deer Creek, we handle launch and docking, and you enjoy 90 minutes of heat, cold plunges, and mountain views.
